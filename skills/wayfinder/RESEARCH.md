# Research brief

You are a sub-agent resolving one **research** ticket of a wayfinder map: the ticket file whose path you were given. Read it in full, then `MAP.md` beside its `tickets/` directory for the destination and the notes the decision is read against.

1. **Investigate the question against primary sources**: official docs, source code, specs, first-party APIs, never a secondary write-up of them. Follow every claim back to the source that owns it.
2. **Write the findings** to `docs/maps/<slug>/research/<NN>-<slug>.md`, named after the ticket. Open with the answer to the ticket's question in a few lines, then the findings, each claim with its source, because a reader zooming from the map wants the answer first and the evidence on demand.
3. **Return the answer and the file's path**, and write nothing else: the session that dispatched you records the Resolution on the ticket and the gist on the map, because several researchers run at once and the map is one file.
